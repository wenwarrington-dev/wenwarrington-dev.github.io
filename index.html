Services

What I can help you with.

Every engagement is different. Here is where I typically work.

01
Fractional COO Support
For businesses that need experienced operational leadership without hiring a full-time COO. I help leaders clarify priorities, organize projects, improve internal systems, manage execution, strengthen communication, and keep the business moving.
Can include: operating rhythms, team meetings, accountability structures, workflow design, vendor coordination, planning, implementation, and ongoing strategic support.
Best for
Founders, small businesses, growing teams, consultants, training companies, nonprofits, and organizations with too much happening in too many places.
02
Process Improvement & Workflow Design
When work feels clunky, slow, confusing, or dependent on one person remembering everything, the process needs attention. I help identify where work is getting stuck, simplify the steps, clarify roles, reduce rework, and create systems that make the business easier to run.
Can include: SOPs, checklists, client onboarding flows, internal workflows, team handoffs, project trackers, service delivery processes, and operational playbooks.
Best for
Businesses dealing with repeated confusion, manual workarounds, inconsistent delivery, team bottlenecks, or "we keep reinventing the wheel" problems.
03
Creative Assets & Business Tools
A lot of businesses have good ideas trapped in messy documents, half-built slides, scattered notes, or someone's brain. I help turn those ideas into polished, usable assets.
Can include: one-pagers, slide decks, training materials, workbooks, pitch decks, proposals, client-facing guides, onboarding materials, lead magnets, social media assets, internal playbooks, and website copy.
Best for
Leaders who need their ideas translated into something clear, professional, and ready to share.
04
App & Digital Tool Development
Sometimes the thing your business needs is not another spreadsheet. I help conceptualize, design, and build practical digital tools that support your business, service delivery, learning experience, or client engagement.
Can include: simple apps, interactive tools, client portals, calculators, workflow helpers, and digital experiences. For PeopleGro, I created a custom app turning a consulting concept into an interactive, scalable tool.
Best for
Consultants, training companies, coaches, service businesses, and organizations that want to turn expertise into a usable digital product or internal tool.
05
Team & Leadership Development
Operations are not only systems. They are also people. I help teams improve communication, clarify expectations, navigate change, reduce friction, and work together more effectively.
Can include: team sessions, leadership workshops, communication labs, I-OPT-informed team development, change leadership support, and practical tools for better conversations.
Best for
Teams that are working hard but struggling with alignment, accountability, collaboration, or communication.
06
Service Packaging & Pricing
Many businesses know what they do, but struggle to package it clearly. I help turn expertise into structured services, programs, workshops, courses, tools, or products that are easy to explain, sell, and deliver.
Can include: naming offers, creating service descriptions, designing delivery models, developing pricing logic, building client journeys, and creating the assets needed to sell and deliver the work.
Best for
Consultants, coaches, trainers, small businesses, and founders who need to make their work easier to explain, sell, and scale.
AI & Your Business

Most AI initiatives fail. Here is why.

Businesses invest in the tools. They map the processes. They build the workflows.

And then nothing changes.

Because they skipped the part that actually determines whether any of it works: the people. How humans respond to change. What drives adoption. Why teams resist new systems even when those systems are better. How to build the trust and behavior shifts that make technology actually stick.

That is my lane. I bring the human behavior and organizational development expertise that most technology implementations completely overlook. And it is the difference between an AI initiative that transforms your business and one that becomes an expensive shelf product.

If your team is exploring AI tools, starting an implementation, or trying to figure out why the last one did not work, let's talk.
